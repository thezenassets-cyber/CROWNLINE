# Image and font assets

## Photography

Photographs in `public/images/` and the hero clip in `public/video/port-operations.mp4`
were produced for this project. They do not depict Crownline Express staff,
facilities, vehicles or customers, and page copy must not describe them as
such. The public site does not carry an image credit line. Replace them with
licensed photography of real operations when that material exists.

Each image exists as a JPEG original and a WebP version (generated by
`npm run assets:optimize`, max width 1600 px, quality 74). Pages use a
`<picture>` element with the WebP source and JPEG fallback, explicit
dimensions, and lazy loading below the fold.

| File (`public/images/…`) | Used on | Alt text approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hero-container-port-blue-hour` | Home hero still and video poster, social preview | Decorative (`alt=""`) |
| `public/video/port-operations.mp4` | Home hero background, muted and looping | Decorative |
| `service-final-mile-delivery` | Domestic parcel service | Descriptive |

| `service-air-freight-loading` | Air freight service | Descriptive |
| `service-ocean-freight-vessel` | Ocean freight service | Descriptive |
| `service-road-freight-highway` | Road freight service | Descriptive |
| `service-business-shipping-packing` | Business shipping service; home business section | Descriptive |

| `tracking-operations-control-room` | About page header background | Decorative (`alt=""`) |

Service cards on the home page and services index reuse the service images.

Image guidelines followed: natural light and realistic scenes; no logos of real
carriers, airlines or shipping lines; no legible fake documents, licence
numbers or signatures; no map with a "live" parcel position.

## Fonts

Self-hosted (no third-party requests, allowed by the `font-src 'self'` CSP):

- **Inter** (variable, Latin subset) — interface and body text.
- **Source Serif 4** (variable, Latin subset) — headings.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and are copied from the
`@fontsource-variable/*` packages into `public/fonts/` by
`npm run assets:optimize`.

## Icons and logo

The crown mark (`public/favicon.svg` and the inline brand mark) and all
interface icons (`src/views/icons.ts`) are simple original SVGs drawn for this
project.
